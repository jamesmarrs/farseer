# Farseer cellular BOM

Bill of materials for the cellular circuit drawn in `cellular-schematic.pdf`
(generated by `gen_cellular_schematic.py`). Design rationale, the full 52-pin
socket map, and datasheet citations live in
[cellular-support-circuit.md](cellular-support-circuit.md) — this file is the
**single source of truth for parts and values**; that one explains *why*.

Scope: everything the host board provides for a **BG95-M3 Mini PCIe card**, fed
from the `3V3_CELL` rail designed in [power-supply-bom.md](power-supply-bom.md).

Authoritative source:
`datasheets/Quectel_BG95-M3_Mini_PCIe_Hardware_Design_V1.0.pdf` — the **only**
document with the 52-pin edge-connector assignment and the card's power domains.
Do **not** use `quectel_bg95_series_hardware_design_v1-6.pdf`: that describes the
raw 102-pin LGA module inside the card, whose pin numbers and 1.8 V levels do not
apply at this socket.

**Status column** matches `power-supply-bom.md`: **Req** mandatory, **Rec**
strongly recommended, **Opt** genuine choice.

> Three things that are **not** on our BOM because they are integrated on the
> card: the 3.8 V VBAT regulation, the RF/BB decoupling and VBAT star network,
> and the antenna connector. Section 1 of `cellular-support-circuit.md` covers
> why. `J2`, `C10`–`C15`, and `J3` are also listed in `power-supply-bom.md` §4 —
> **same physical parts**, do not order twice.
>
> [full-bom.md](full-bom.md) merges all four BOMs into one ordering list with
> that overlap already resolved. Regenerate it with
> `python3 docs/gen_full_bom.py` after any change here.

## 1. Mini PCIe socket and rail decoupling

§3.2 governs: *"In 2G network, the input peak current may reach **2.7 A**...
a **low-ESR bypass capacitor no less than 470 µF** should be used."* Quectel's
Figure 3 reference puts 470 µF + 100 nF + 33 pF + 10 pF at `VCC_3V3`.

Placement constraint from §3.2 that is decided at layout, not at order time: keep
buck A's inductor, catch diode, and switch-node copper **away from the antenna
connector and cable run**. For thermal sizing, LTE Cat M1 TX is far gentler
(235–260 mA avg, 495–618 mA max, Table 21) — **the 2.7 A figure is the 2G/EGPRS
case**, and it is what sets the rail.

## 2. (U)SIM interface — §3.3, Figure 4

Only **1.8 V (U)SIM cards are supported**; the card supplies `USIM_VDD` itself.
Everything here belongs **at the holder**, not at the socket. Built from
**Figure 4 (holder with card-detect switch)** — the detect switch is wired to
`USIM_DET` (pin 44) for hot-plug recovery, so the 6-pin Figure 5 variant no
longer applies. The holder is a **hinged type** chosen for in-car vibration:
the stainless cover locks over the card (lock force ≥ 1.5 N, unlock ≤ 10 N;
shock-tested at 490 m/s² and vibration-tested per the GCT spec), unlike a
push-push holder whose eject spring a hard shock can actuate. The SIM itself is
a **Hologram Hyper triple-cut card punched to the nano (4FF) cut** — 1.62–5.5 V,
so 1.8 V-compatible, industrial grade −40 to +105 °C.

| Ref | Qty | Status | Value / description | Suggested MPN (datasheet) | Notes |
|-----|-----|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| J3 | 1 | Req | Nano-SIM holder, **hinged locking cover**, 6 contacts + detect switch | **GCT SIM8060-6-1-14-00-A** | Normally-open switch closes `SW` to the grounded shell when the card is seated → `SIM_DET` low = inserted |
| C_sim1 | 1 | Req | **100 nF**, 0402, `USIM_VDD` → GND | Murata GRM155R71C104KA88D | §3.3: decoupling **must not exceed 1 µF** and must sit close to the holder |
| C_sim2..4 | 3 | Req | **33 pF**, 0402, one per RST / CLK / DATA to GND | Murata GRM1555C1H330JA01D | Filters EGSM900 interference |

Deliberate deviation from Figure 4: the 0 Ω series links Quectel draws in
RST / CLK / DATA (a cut/isolate debug aid, no electrical function) are
**omitted** — the lines run straight from the socket to the holder.
| D_sim | 1 | Req | TVS array, **parasitic capacitance ≤ 15 pF** | **TI TPD4E02B04DQAR** (~0.25 pF/ch, USON-10) | At the holder, on VDD / IO / CLK / RST. The 15 pF ceiling is the selection criterion |
| R_det1, R_det2 | 2 | Req | **51 kΩ**, 0402 — `SIM_DET` divider from `3V3_CELL` | Yageo RC0402FR-0751KL | See detect note below |
| R_simPU | 1 | Opt | **15 kΩ** 0402 to `USIM_VDD` | Yageo RC0402FR-0715KL | Pull-up on `USIM_DATA`; improves anti-jamming on long or noisy traces. Value per Figure 4/5; fit **DNP** by default |

**Card-detect divider (R_det1/R_det2).** Quectel's Figure 4 pulls `USIM_DET` up
to an always-on **1.8 V** rail through 51 kΩ, but the Mini PCIe card exports no
such rail — only `USIM_VDD`, which the modem may power down when no SIM is
present, which would corrupt the detect level exactly when it matters. A
51 k / 51 k divider from `3V3_CELL` synthesises ~1.65 V on `SIM_DET`, inside
the 1.2–2.0 V VIH window of the 1.8 V domain (Table 22/23); the holder switch
shorts the node to GND when a card is seated. Hot-plug is **disabled by
default** (§3.3) — firmware enables it once with `AT+QSIMDET=1,0` (low =
inserted; saved to NV, effective after reboot) plus `AT+QSIMSTAT=1` for removal
URCs.

The SIM holder is the **only user-accessible connector** on this subsystem, so it
is the realistic ESD entry point and the one place protection is mandatory.
Layout rules (all pass/fail at review): trace length **< 200 mm**, ground between
socket and holder **≥ 0.5 mm wide**, `USIM_DATA` and `USIM_CLK` separated and
shielded by ground, and the whole group kept away from RF and the switching rail.

## 3. UART to the PIC — §3.5

Direct connection, **3.3 V both ends, no level shifters anywhere**. Default
115200 8N1, matching [uart3.c](../uart3.c).

| Ref | Qty | Status | Value / description | Suggested MPN (datasheet) | Notes |
|-----|-----|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| R_u3a, R_u3b | 2 | Rec | 220 Ω 0603 | generic | Series on `CELL_RX` / `CELL_TX`. Both ends now share a board, so the back-power case from [bench-wiring.md](bench-wiring.md) is gone; these remain useful for edge-rate and ESD control |

Nets, no parts: PIC RF4 (pin 12, U3TX) → socket pin **11**; socket pin **13** →
PIC RF5 (pin 13, U3RX). The signal names in Table 4 are **card-relative and
already crossed** — do not cross them a second time. `UART_CTS` (23) and
`UART_RTS` (25) are Opt; flow control is off by default and firmware does not use
it, but route them to spare pins if the budget allows since they cannot be
retrofitted.

## 4. Control and indication

| Ref | Qty | Status | Value / description | Suggested MPN (datasheet) | Notes |
|-----|-----|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| R_perst | 1 | Rec | 10 kΩ 0603 to `3V3_SYS` | generic | Belt-and-braces pull-up on `PERST#` (pin 22); holds reset released while the PIC is itself in reset. The card already pulls up |
| TP_perst | 1 | Rec | Test point | generic | A 2–3.8 s reset pulse is worth being able to scope |
| D_wwan | 1 | Opt | LED, low-current, 0603 | generic | `LED_WWAN#` (pin 42), open collector, active low |
| R_wwan | 1 | **Req if D_wwan fitted** | **1 kΩ** 0603 from `3V3_SYS` | generic | §3.7.4: *"a resistor must be placed in series with the LED."* ≈2 mA at 3.3 V. **Do not omit** — the pin sinks up to 40 mA and will cook the LED |

`PERST#` is the only reset available. Drive it from a PIC GPIO, **open-drain**
(toggle `TRIS` against the card's pull-up) so the idle state is defined while the
PIC is in reset. Reset needs a low of **≥ 2 s and ≤ 3.8 s**, at **VIL ≤ 0.45 V** —
tighter than the general 3.3 V I/O spec. Implement that delay in the existing
non-blocking state machine in [bg95.c](../bg95.c), never as a busy-wait.

The default LED pattern is a genuinely useful trackside diagnostic — 200 ms
low / 1800 ms high is *searching*, the inverse is *registered idle*, and
125/125 ms is *data transfer*. Worth the two parts.

Left NC by design: `W_DISABLE#` (20, function disabled by default and `AT+CFUN=4`
does the same job) and all PCM and I2C pins. `USIM_DET` (44) is **no longer NC**
— it is wired to the SIM holder's detect switch (§2) for hot-plug recovery.
`RI` (17) and `DTR` (31) are Opt — routing `RI` to an interrupt-on-change pin
would let firmware stop polling for downlink URCs.

## 5. USB — Opt, §3.4

Not needed for operation, but it is the **firmware-upgrade path** for the modem.
Fitting a header means the card can be re-flashed without desoldering.

| Ref | Qty | Status | Value / description | Suggested MPN (datasheet) | Notes |
|-----|-----|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| L_usb | 1 | Rec | Common-mode choke, USB 2.0 rated | Würth WE-CNSW or equivalent | §3.4 recommends one in series for EMI. **Place close to the socket** |
| D_usb | 1 | Rec | Low-capacitance ESD array, 2-line | select for low C on a 90 Ω pair | Per Figure 6 |
| R_usb1, R_usb2 | 2 | Opt | 0 Ω 0402, **not mounted by default** | generic | Series to test points, per Figure 6 |
| J_usb | 1 | Opt | USB connector or 4-pin header | generic | Firmware-update access |

Routing: **90 Ω differential** with ground surround, not under crystals,
oscillators, magnetics, or RF traces, preferably on an inner layer, stubs
minimal.

## Verify before ordering

- [ ] All four `VCC_3V3` pins (2, 39, 41, 52) fed, all fourteen GND pins to plane
- [ ] Every RESERVED pin genuinely **unconnected** — not grounded (Table 4 note 3)
- [ ] Reservoir is **≥ 470 µF with effective ESR ≤ 10 mΩ** at the socket
- [ ] No level shifters anywhere on the UART — the card is 3.3 V at this connector
      (Table 8)
- [ ] PIC TX → pin **11**, PIC RX → pin **13** (names are card-relative; do not
      cross twice)
- [ ] `PERST#` drive meets **VIL ≤ 0.45 V**, and firmware holds it low 2–3.8 s
- [ ] `LED_WWAN#` has its 1 kΩ series resistor if the LED is fitted
- [ ] SIM: 100 nF ≤ 1 µF at holder, 3× 33 pF, TVS ≤ 15 pF, GND ≥ 0.5 mm,
      trace < 200 mm, DATA and CLK separated and shielded (0 Ω series links
      deliberately omitted)
- [ ] `USIM_DET` wired to the holder switch through the 51 k / 51 k divider;
      firmware sends `AT+QSIMDET=1,0` (polarity must match the wiring)
- [ ] PCM and I2C left NC
- [ ] Buck A switch node and inductor kept away from the antenna connector and
      cable route (§3.2 EMI warning)
- [ ] `3V3_CELL` still **3.3 V nominal** — see cellular-support-circuit.md §3
      before anyone "adds margin"
- [ ] Socket latch **plus** secondary retention for vibration
- [ ] Rebuild the drawing with `./docs/build_schematics.sh cellular` after any
      change here
